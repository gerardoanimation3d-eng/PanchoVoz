PanchoVoz — paquete de voz de "Pancho y Pancha" (mod de Minecraft de Gerardeex)
=============================================================================

Este paquete es un programa aparte del mod. El mod lo abre en tu PC y le pide frases por 127.0.0.1 (nada sale
de tu PC). Se cierra solo cuando cierras Minecraft.

This package is a separate program from the mod. The mod starts it on your PC and asks it for sentences over
127.0.0.1 (nothing leaves your PC). It closes by itself when you close Minecraft.
